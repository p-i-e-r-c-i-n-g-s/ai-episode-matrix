# Director and movie mode example

## Request

Create a new image-base prompt for an original arctic research team discovering an impossible signal. Use movie mode as a 1980s practical-effects science-fiction homage, with Carpenter-style spatial suspense as a secondary influence.

## Style controls

```text
RELATION: homage
LOCKED: original research team, arctic station, impossible signal, no existing characters or dialogue
INFLUENCED: horizontal ensemble geography, weaponized negative space, practical light sources, deep blue exterior, slow-burn reveal
FLEXIBLE: exact wardrobe, set dressing, signal design, focal length, weather intensity
```

## Image-base prompt

A cinematic film still of an original four-person research team inside a cramped arctic communications room at night, arranged horizontally across a wide frame so every eyeline and doorway remains readable. A small analog receiver glows on a metal desk while a dark corridor recedes behind them; the impossible signal is suggested by the receiver’s unnatural pulse, not shown as a creature. Practical illumination only: cold blue light from the snow-covered windows, a weak warm desk lamp, and hard white spill from a monitor, leaving deep negative space in the corridor. Restrained 1980s practical-effects science-fiction atmosphere, tactile worn production design, damp wool, frost, cables, brushed steel, controlled high contrast, desaturated earth tones, subtle fine grain, wide anamorphic perspective, slow-burn suspense, original characters and setting.
