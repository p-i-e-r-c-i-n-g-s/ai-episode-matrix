# Image-to-video example

## Request

Animate an image of a detective in a rain-soaked alley. Keep the character and composition stable. Use a restrained Carpenter-inspired suspense grammar.

## Prompt

Use the supplied image as the visual source of truth. Preserve the detective’s identity, facial structure, coat, hat, wet alley geometry, camera height, wide framing, midnight-blue palette, practical sodium-vapor light, and existing shadow placement. Animate one primary action: the detective slowly turns their head toward a distant sound, then holds. Add subtle secondary motion: rain falling consistently, coat fabric responding to the turn, a faint breath plume, and a small reflection ripple in a puddle. Camera: slow, level lateral track to the right, detached and stabilized, ending in the same wide spatial relationship. Maintain screen direction and physical contact. No scene change, no new characters, no prop substitutions, no facial drift, no extra limbs, no rubbery motion, no unwanted zoom, no cuts, no text, no flicker.

## Success criteria

- Detective identity and original composition remain stable.
- Head turn is readable and completes naturally.
- Rain, coat, breath, and reflection move without distracting deformation.
- Camera movement is slow, level, and spatially motivated.
