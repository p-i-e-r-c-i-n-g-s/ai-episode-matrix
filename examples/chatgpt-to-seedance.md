# ChatGPT Image Gen → Seedance example

## Step 1 — ChatGPT Image Gen

Create a cinematic still of Mara in the flooded underground station at night. Preserve her approved charcoal coat, damaged receiver in her right hand, shoulder-length dark hair, and the blue emergency light reflecting in shallow water. Medium-wide composition, eye-level 35mm perspective, restrained naturalistic drama, rainwater dripping from the ceiling, clear silhouette and readable hands. This is the approved start-frame candidate for EP-05 / SC-03 / SH-02.

Record the selected result as `REF-EP05-SC03-SH02_v01` only after visual review.

## Step 2 — Seedance image-to-video

```text
REFERENCE: Use the supplied image as the visual source of truth. One continuous shot.
PRESERVE: Mara's identity, charcoal coat, damaged receiver in her right hand, flooded station geometry, eye-level medium-wide framing, 35mm perspective, blue emergency light, naturalistic dramatic medium, and existing reflections.
TRANSFORM: Mara slowly turns her head toward a distant pulse of light. Her grip tightens once around the receiver. Ceiling water continues dripping and creates small physically consistent ripples. Camera makes one slow, level push-in without changing the spatial relationship.
TIMING / END STATE: Begin still; head turn over the first half; receiver pulse at the action peak; settle on Mara looking toward the light; end before any cut or location change.
AUDIO (OPTIONAL): Quiet station room tone, water drips, one restrained electrical pulse; no dialogue unless an approved audio asset is supplied.
AVOID: Face or wardrobe drift, receiver substitution, new characters, geometry warping, extra limbs, unwanted cuts, flicker, rubbery water, text, or style change.
```

## Step 3 — Review and record

Check identity, receiver hand, screen direction, light continuity, action readability, camera stability, and audio timing. Save the result as a generated take, record observed failures, and approve it before it becomes the next shot's source of truth.
