# Compact film-language guide

- 18–24mm: expansive, spatial, energetic; foreground exaggeration.
- 28–35mm: naturalistic environmental perspective; useful for embodied movement.
- 50mm: neutral, intimate, human-scale perspective.
- 75–100mm: compressed space, portrait isolation, restrained observation.
- Wide shot: geography and relationship; medium: behavior and interaction; close-up: thought, reaction, or detail.
- Dolly/push-in: attention or emotional commitment. Pull-back: reveal, isolation, or release. Lateral track: parallel observation or kinetic relation. Handheld: embodied instability; specify restrained or documentary rather than generic “shaky.”
- Hard cut: decisive change or impact. Match cut: visual/action continuity. J-cut/L-cut: sound leads or trails picture. Dissolve: time, memory, or soft transition; do not use by default.
- “Golden hour” is insufficient alone: specify low backlight, warm rim, long shadows, lifted fill, and exposure priority.
