# Image-to-video motion grammar

Design motion in layers: primary subject action; secondary motion (hair, cloth, breath, props); camera motion; environmental motion; and temporal behavior (acceleration, hold, loop, reveal, impact).

Use `start state → initiation → action peak → settle/end state`. State what must not move. Specify physical causality: feet maintain contact, shadows follow light, cloth responds to motion, reflections remain coherent, and screen direction is preserved.
